Edge-notch card visualizations

Edge-notch cards (McBee Keysort and kin) store data in holes punched around the rim of an index card. To encode a value you notch a hole open to the edge; to retrieve records you slide a needle through a position and lift — the notched cards fall away from the deck.

These interactive demos explore a real deck: what each notch position encodes, and how the physical sort actually plays out.

Decode · by position

Position explorer

Hover any notch position to reveal what it encodes — specialty codes from the original punch-code key (with each code's status and evidence), the NYC proximity holes, 3-needle state & city codes, surname and company initials. Paired positions light up in matching colors.
