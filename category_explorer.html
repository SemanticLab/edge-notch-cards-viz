Hover any top-edge label, e.g. physics or holography.

Top-edge codes come from the original punch-code key (transcribed in scripts/data/punch_code_key.json and validated against the cards — see KEY_VALIDATION.md). Label weight shows each code's standing: solid black = active & confirmed by the card data, dark gray = active with weaker evidence, italic gray = consolidated into another code (circled on the key), light italic = retired or spare. Hover a label for its card-system code, status, and evidence.

The other edges follow Rosenboom's 1968 notes (EDGES_V2.md): the top-right trio is the NYC proximity code, the bottom edge holds 3-needle state & city codes, the right edge the surname's first letter and the left edge the company's first letter (both fuzzy two-hole ladders) plus the informal "good guys" marker.
